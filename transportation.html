Getting to and around Taniti

Visitors can reach Taniti by air or cruise ship and explore the island by bus, taxi, rental car, bicycle, or on foot.

Arriving on the island

Most visitors arrive by air. Taniti’s small airport accommodates small jets and propeller planes. An expansion is underway to allow larger jets within the next few years.

Rental cars

Rental cars are available from a local rental agency near the airport.

By bus

Public buses serve Taniti City daily from 5 a.m. to 11 p.m. Private buses serve the rest of the island.

By taxi

Taxis are available in Taniti City.

Walking and Bicycles

Taniti City is fairly flat and very walkable. The area surrounding Merriton Landing is also easy to explore on foot. Bicycles and helmets are available to rent from several vendors. Wearing a helmet is required by law. Explore attractions and activities
