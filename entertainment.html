Things to do

Enjoy Taniti’s beaches, explore its rainforest, visit the active volcano, and discover local attractions and recreation.Here are some activities and attractions you can enjoy in Taniti:

Beaches and Taniti City

Taniti City features native architecture and nearby white, sandy beaches surrounding Yellow Leaf Bay.

Rainforest and volcano

Explore the rainforest on a hike, try zip-lining, or visit Taniti’s small, active volcano.

Local History and Art

Visit the local history museum and explore several art galleries.

Tours and recreation

Activities include boat and bus tours, chartered fishing tours, snorkeling, helicopter rides, bowling, and an arcade. Visitors can also enjoy pubs, a microbrewery, a dance club, and a movie theater.

Merriton Landing

Many entertainment activities are located in Merriton Landing, a growing area on the north side of Yellow Leaf Bay. The surrounding area is easy to explore on foot.
