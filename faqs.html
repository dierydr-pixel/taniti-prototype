Frequently Asked Questions

Find practical information about currency, electricity, local rules, language, healthcare, and safety to help you plan your visit to Taniti.

What currency is used in Taniti?

Taniti uses the U.S. dollar. Many businesses also accept euros and yen. Several banks offer currency exchange, and many businesses accept major credit cards.

What electrical outlets are used in Taniti?

Power outlets are 120 volts, the same as in the United States.

What is the drinking age in Taniti?

The drinking age is 18. The island information notes that this rule is not strictly enforced.

When can alcohol be purchased or served?

Alcohol cannot be sold or served between midnight and 9 a.m.

Is English widely spoken in Taniti?

Many younger Tanitians speak fluent English. Very little English is spoken in rural areas, especially by older residents.

Safety and holidays

Violent crime is very rare. However, reports of pickpocketing and other petty crimes have increased as tourism grows.Taniti observes many national holidays. Many tourist attractions and restaurants close on holidays, so visitors should plan accordingly.

Restaurants and Groceries

Taniti has ten restaurants: five primarily serve local fish and rice, three offer American-style meals, and two serve Pan-Asian cuisine. The island also has two supermarkets, two smaller grocery stores, and a convenience store open 24 hours a day.
